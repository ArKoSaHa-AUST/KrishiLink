# Page: Landing / Home

## Purpose
First page any visitor sees. Introduces KrishiLink and pushes visitors toward registering as a Farmer, Equipment Owner, or Godown Owner.

## Layout
- **Top navbar**: Logo (left), nav links (Home, How it Works, About), Login / Register buttons (right)
- **Hero section**: Short tagline ("Rent equipment, book storage, get crop advice — all in one place"), background image of farmland, primary CTA button "Get Started"
- **"How it Works" strip**: 3 icon cards side by side — Rent Equipment / Book Storage / Get Crop Advice, each with a one-line description
- **Role selection cards**: 3 clickable cards (Farmer, Equipment Owner, Godown Owner) that route to a pre-filled registration form
- **Stats/trust strip** (optional): e.g. "X equipment listed, Y godowns available" — placeholder numbers acceptable for MVP
- **Footer**: quick links, contact info, social icons

## Workflow
1. Visitor lands on page → scans hero + how-it-works section
2. Clicks a role card or "Get Started" → routes to Register page (role pre-selected if from role card)
3. Returning users click "Login" in navbar

## Interactions
- Smooth scroll for "How it Works" anchor link in navbar
- Hover states on role cards (slight lift/shadow)
- Sticky navbar on scroll
- Mobile: navbar collapses into hamburger menu

## Design Notes
- Warm, agricultural color palette (greens/earth tones), not corporate blue
- Large tap targets for mobile (farmers may access via phone)
- Keep hero copy short — assume some users have low digital literacy
