# Page: Godown Browse / Search (Farmer) - Part 3: Design & Styling Notes

## Design Notes
- **UI Theme**: Agriculture-related green and white color palette — mirrors equipment browse layout with clean white cards, soft green borders, and nature-green action elements
- **Component Consistency**: Reuse the same card/grid/filter component pattern as Equipment Browse — keeps the app feeling consistent and reduces the learning curve between the two browse flows
- **Status & Media**: Availability badges with unified dashboard styling; prominent warehouse/cold storage images with consistent 16:10 aspect ratio and subtle hover zoom
