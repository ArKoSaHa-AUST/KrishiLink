# Page: Booking / Transaction History (Farmer) - Part 3: Design, Clarity & Timeline Notes

## Design Notes
- **UI Theme**: Agriculture-related green and white color palette — crisp white table/card backgrounds with subtle green headers, green navigation pills/tabs, and agricultural green status indicators
- **Clarity & Trust**: Farmers should be able to tell at a glance what's happening with each request (dates, rates, owner contact, action status)
- **Status Timeline**: Keep status timeline clean and step-based: Requested → Accepted/Rejected → Completed
