# Page: Equipment Owner Dashboard - Part 1: Overview & Layout

## Purpose
Landing page for an Equipment Owner after login — overview of their listings and pending requests.

## Layout
- **Top navbar**: Logo, My Listings, Requests, Profile icon
- **Summary cards row**: Total Listings, Active Rentals, Pending Requests (counts, clickable)
- **"Add New Equipment" button**: prominent, top-right of the listings section
- **My listings preview**: grid/list of owner's equipment with status (Available/Rented/Unavailable), quick "Edit" and "Availability" actions
- **Pending requests widget**: list of incoming rental requests awaiting Accept/Reject, with farmer name, equipment, requested dates
